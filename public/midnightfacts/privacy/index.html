Privacy Policy

OpenClaw Shorts (used by MidnightFacts) · Last updated: 21 October 2026 (TikTok section added)

OpenClaw Shorts is a private tool operated by Andrii Kozakov to upload short videos to his own MidnightFacts accounts on YouTube and TikTok. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Google and TikTok user data the app accesses and how it is handled. See also the terms of service.

Google / YouTube data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these permissions:

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload videos the owner has reviewed and approved.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel is connected and to check the status of uploaded videos.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read performance numbers (views, watch time, likes, subscribers gained) for the owner's own videos and build the owner's weekly performance report.

The app does not access email, contacts, files, or any other Google data.

TikTok data the app accesses

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app requests these permissions:

  • Upload videos as drafts (video.upload): to send approved videos to the owner's TikTok inbox, where the owner edits and publishes them in the TikTok app.
  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): to show which TikTok account is connected (open ID and display name).
  • Public video list (video.list): to read view, like, comment and share counts of the owner's own public videos for the weekly performance report.
  • Profile stats (user.info.stats): to read the owner's follower and like totals for the same report.

The app does not publish to TikTok directly and does not read messages, followers' identities, private videos or any other TikTok data.

How the data is used and stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's private server, readable only by the account that runs the tool.
  • They are used solely to upload approved videos to the owner's accounts, read their upload status, and read the owner's own performance numbers.
  • To write the owner's weekly report, those performance numbers (with the owner's own video titles) are sent to an AI model API (Google Gemini) for analysis. The report goes only to the owner.
  • No Google or TikTok user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. You can revoke Google access at any time in your Google Account third-party connections, and TikTok access in the TikTok app's settings for connected apps; the stored token then stops working and is deleted from the server. Videos sent to the TikTok inbox are handled by TikTok under the TikTok Privacy Policy.
