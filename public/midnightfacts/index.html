MidnightFacts

Short science and nature facts on YouTube.

MidnightFacts is a personal project by Andrii Kozakov. Short vertical videos are drafted with AI tools (script, visuals and narration), checked for accuracy, and reviewed and approved by a person before they are published on the MidnightFacts YouTube channel.

The "OpenClaw Shorts" app connected to this channel is used only by its owner to upload approved videos to MidnightFacts. It is not offered to other users.
